Subscription plans

Plan payments are not refundable once your card is active, except for duplicate charges or a proven failure of the service.

NFC card orders

  • Before design work starts: full refund of the cards and delivery.
  • After design starts, before printing: 50% of the card price is refunded.
  • After printing starts: the order cannot be cancelled or refunded.
  • Faulty chip: a card whose NFC chip does not work, reported within 30 days of delivery, is replaced free.
  • Lost or damaged cards: reorder at the standard price. The new card opens the same permanent link.

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Paystack. Your bank or network may take a few working days to show them.

How to ask

Cancel from your order page, or contact support with your order number or payment reference.
