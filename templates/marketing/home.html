About AD

Made by a design studio that lives in print.

AD Smart Business Cards is a service of AD Graphics, a Ghanaian design studio behind more than 2,500 designs — logos, flyers, brand kits and print for businesses, churches, schools and events.

We built AD Smart so your contact details work as hard as your brand does: always up to date, easy to share, and saved in one tap. Every NFC card is designed by our team, printed, encoded with your permanent link and tap-tested on both Android and iPhone before it leaves the studio.
